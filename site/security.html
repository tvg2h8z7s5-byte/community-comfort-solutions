Security

Last updated: September 2026. Have this reviewed before relying on it.

We take care with the information customers share with us. This page describes how this website handles your information and how to reach us about a security concern.

Using this website

Messages sent through our contact and scheduling forms are delivered to our business email by a third-party form service. This website does not take payments or store customer accounts, and we do not ask you to submit card numbers, bank details, or Social Security numbers through it.

Please do not include sensitive information such as passwords or payment details in a form or an email. If we need anything like that, we will discuss the right way to handle it with you by phone.

Report a security concern

If you believe you have found a problem with this website, or you received a message that appears to come from us but looks suspicious, email contact@communitycomfortsolutions.org or call 917-608-3201. Please include what you saw and how to reproduce it, and we will look into it.
