Homeowner Resources

General tips to help you look after your heating and cooling equipment. These are not a substitute for your equipment manufacturer's instructions or a service visit.

Routine care

  • Check your air filter regularly and replace or clean it according to your manufacturer's guidance.
  • Keep the area around outdoor units clear of leaves, snow, and debris.
  • Keep supply and return vents unblocked by furniture and curtains.
  • Schedule seasonal tune-ups before the heating and cooling seasons. See our maintenance page.

When to call for service

  • No heat or no cooling
  • Unusual noises, odors, or frequent cycling
  • Uneven temperatures between rooms
  • Water leaking near indoor equipment

Safety

If you smell gas or your carbon monoxide alarm sounds, leave the building right away and call 911 or your gas utility from outside. Do not wait for a service appointment.

For anything else, call 917-608-3201 or request service.
